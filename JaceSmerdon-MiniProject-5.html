Four places to begin

Where to next?

A different feeling in every direction.

Kyoto, Japan

Old streets, soft lantern light, and a slower pace.

A city where peaceful gardens and wooden tea houses sit alongside lively markets and modern neighborhoods.

Things to do

  • Walk the Fushimi Inari gates
  • Visit Arashiyama
  • Try a tea ceremony

Little detail: Kyoto is famous for its seasonal cherry blossoms and autumn leaves.

Rome, Italy

Ancient landmarks around every corner.

A lively open-air history book, with grand ruins, neighborhood cafes, and plenty of unforgettable food.

Things to do

  • Tour the Colosseum
  • See the Trevi Fountain
  • Explore Trastevere

Little detail: Rome's historic center is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Iceland

Big landscapes made for the long way around.

Waterfalls, black-sand beaches, and volcanic landscapes make every drive feel like an expedition.

Things to do

  • See the Golden Circle
  • Relax in a hot spring
  • Chase the northern lights

Little detail: Iceland sits where the North American and Eurasian tectonic plates meet.

New York, USA

Neighborhoods with their own rhythm.

A fast-moving city of skyline views, world-class museums, neighborhood slices, and late-night energy.

Things to do

  • Walk Central Park
  • Visit a museum
  • See the skyline at dusk

Little detail: The city's five boroughs each have a distinct character.

Featured destination · Japan

Take the scenic route through Kyoto.

Kyoto rewards unhurried exploring. Start early at the shrine paths, pause for lunch in a small neighborhood spot, then spend the afternoon among gardens and old lanes. The best moments often happen between the famous stops.

Travel tip: Visit popular sites early in the morning, and leave time to wander the quieter side streets.
