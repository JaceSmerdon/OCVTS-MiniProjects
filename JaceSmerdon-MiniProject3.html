About Us

Sunset Bistro is a family-owned restaurant created to bring people together through fresh food and friendly service. Inspired by modern American cuisine, we focus on quality ingredients and a welcoming atmosphere.

Our mission is simple: provide great food, excellent service, and a comfortable place for everyone to enjoy.
